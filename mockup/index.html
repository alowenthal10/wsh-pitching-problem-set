About this mockup. Bio, team, and season results load live from the MLB Stats API in the viewer's browser when it is reachable; otherwise a synthetic demo pitcher is shown. All Stuff, Location, and Pitching values, pitch shapes, heatmaps, Pitch Lab projections, and biomechanics are illustrative and generated for this mockup. They are not outputs of any real model.

Grade conversion: grade = 50 + 10 × (plus − 100) ÷ SD, clamped to 20–80 and shown in 5-point steps. SDs are placeholders for the R&D analyst to supply per model and level.
