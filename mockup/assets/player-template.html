About this mockup. Bio, rosters, transactions, and season results load live from the MLB Stats API. Pitch data comes from Statcast (Baseball Savant), rebuilt daily by a GitHub Action. Stuff grades use tjStuff+ by Thomas Nestico (MIT License), which also runs in your browser for Pitch Lab. Location and Pitching grades, the heatmap surfaces, and model-expected ERA come from this project's own models, fit on the same Statcast data (method and code). Still mock and tagged: grip presets, the results-adjusted formula, the unmodeled-traits list, and biomechanics. Data © MLB Advanced Media, used for non-commercial purposes.

Grade conversion: grade = 50 + 10 × (plus − 100) ÷ SD, clamped to 20–80 and shown in 5-point steps. SDs are placeholders for the R&D analyst to supply per model and level.
